Common questions

Why am I not getting notifications?
Check that notifications are enabled for LifeScheduler in iOS Settings, and review your device's Focus settings.

Why is voice input unavailable?
Check Microphone and Speech Recognition permissions in iOS Settings. You can enter tasks manually if you prefer.

How do I manage location access?
Open iOS Settings, find LifeScheduler, and change the location permission.

Where is my information stored?
See our Privacy Policy for details about on-device data and Apple system services.
